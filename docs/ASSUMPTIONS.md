# Assumptions

The problem statement is the primary source of truth. **Where it is silent we do not silently invent** — every gap is recorded here with: what the brief says, what we decided, why, and what could change. If you discover a new gap while building, add a row (next free `A-nn`) in your PR and tell the team; do not just code around it.

Legend — *Brief says*: quoted/paraphrased from *Sports Club Management System.pdf*. *Impact*: where a change would ripple.

| ID | Topic | Brief says | Decision | Why | If it changes (impact) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Sports offered | §1 "tennis, padel and badminton club"; §2 "tennis and cricket courts" | Support **four** sports: tennis, cricket, padel, badminton (`sport_type`). Seed: 2 tennis courts, 1 cricket net, 1 padel, 2 badminton | The two passages disagree; supporting all four costs one enum and costs nothing at runtime | Add/remove enum value (migration + enums.ts) |
| A-02 | Club identity & location | Mentions "near them" / no website; gives no address, phone, city | Placeholder address/phone/email in `club_settings` (marked `PLACEHOLDER`) | Homepage needs contact info; real values unknown | Owner edits settings; no code change |
| A-03 | Opening hours | Not specified | 06:00–22:00 IST (`club_open_time`/`club_close_time`); last session starts 21:00 | Needed to generate slots | Settings only |
| A-04 | Prices and benefit values | "Gold (premium, full access), Silver (standard), Junior (under 18, discounted)"; "Members pay less than walk-ins, or nothing at all, depending on their plan" | Seed values: Gold ₹30,000 / 100 % courts / 15 % shop / 15 % bar; Silver ₹15,000 / 50 % / 10 % / 10 %; Junior ₹7,500 / 70 % / 5 % / 5 %. Court walk-in ₹400–1200/h. All **data** (plans/courts), editable | Brief gives the shape, not the numbers; "full access" read as "courts free" | Edit plans/courts via owner UI |
| A-05 | Business clients | "memberships and business clients to invoice" — nothing about who they are or what they buy | A business client is a company that receives **free-form line-item invoices** (e.g. corporate sports day, quarterly net bookings), pays by any method, may have a portal login. **No supplier/vendor workflow** | Do not invent a relationship the brief does not describe | Add structured services → new tables, additive |
| A-06 | Shop products | Sells "rackets, balls, shoes, accessories and apparel" | Modelled as **generic club-shop products** (SKU, brand, category, price, stock) — no distinction between club-branded and third-party, no suppliers/purchase orders | Brief does not say; generic covers both | Add supplier tables later |
| A-07 | Plan change money | "Plans change" | Old term ends yesterday; new term starts today at the **full new-plan price, no pro-rata credit**; history kept | Simplest defensible rule; pro-rata is a business decision | Add credit calculation in `memberships.changePlan` |
| A-08 | Membership length | "Their membership will run out one day" | Plans are 12 months (`duration_months` column allows 1/3/6/12) | Typical; data-driven | Add plan rows |
| A-09 | Junior eligibility | "Junior (under 18, discounted)" | DOB required to buy Junior; age ≤ 17 at term start; term continues if they turn 18; renewal must be non-Junior | Makes "under 18" testable | `plan.max_age`, service check |
| A-10 | Cancellation policy | "people cancel"; no policy | Full refund if cancelled ≥ 2 h before start; none inside (members); staff can override. `cancellation_cutoff_hours` setting | Needed for refunds; configurable | Settings only |
| A-11 | Daily play limit | "each member can play at most twice a day" | Per member per **IST calendar day**; default 2 from `plan.max_plays_per_day` (all three plans = 2); applies to member bookings; guests unlimited; trial/maintenance not counted | Brief states 2 for every member; column keeps it data-driven | Edit plan |
| A-12 | Social play and the limit | Friday social play: "many people share one court" | A joined social spot **counts as a play** toward the daily limit | Prevents a loophole; it is still a session on a court | Remove join from `playsUsed` query (R-COURT-04) |
| A-13 | Social-play format | "on Friday night the courts open up for social play where many people share one court" | Window Fri 18:00–22:00 (setting); each session = one 1-hour slot on one court with a capacity and per-person fee; several sessions per evening; fee = guest fee, members get their court discount (Gold free) | Brief gives no fee/capacity | Settings + session fields |
| A-14 | Online payments | Money arrives "by card, cash and online" | `ONLINE` handled by a **mock gateway** in the hackathon build; real gateway is a later swap (`PAYMENT_PROVIDER`) | 12-hour budget; not a differentiator | ADR-011 |
| A-15 | Tax | "taxes to report" — no regime, no rates | India GST modelled: court/membership/shop prices tax-inclusive at 18 %, bar/cafeteria 5 %, invoices tax-exclusive at 18 %. Rates are settings; no HSN codes, no GST returns, no input-tax credit | Needs *some* rate to produce a tax report; chosen to be plausible and editable | Settings; richer tax model later |
| A-16 | Delivery | "have them delivered" | Flat fee ₹50, free over ₹2000 (settings); address on the order; staff mark `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`/`COMPLETED`; no couriers, tracking or distance rules | Minimum viable delivery | Settings; courier integration later |
| A-17 | Who runs the bar POS | "Staff work in shifts"; roles given by the team: Front Desk, Kitchen Manager (no bar staff role) | **FRONT_DESK operates the bar/cafeteria POS and the shop counter**; KITCHEN_MANAGER only moves orders through the kitchen; shifts have a `BAR` area for rostering | Team fixed the role list; keeps roles to five | Add a role later (enum + permissions) |
| A-18 | Who can order online | "would rather order them from home" (a member) | Online shop ordering requires a **member account** (signup is free); visitors browse only | Delivery needs identity; discount needs a member | Allow guest checkout later |
| A-19 | Bar ordering by members | "Members expect their discount without having to ask" | Members **do not self-order** bar items in v1; staff place orders and members see their orders/tabs read-only | Orders are taken at the table | Add member ordering later |
| A-20 | Alcohol / age checks | "bar and cafeteria" | Out of scope (R-BAR-10); seed menu has no alcohol; no age gating | Licensing varies and isn't in the brief | Add flags per item |
| A-21 | Walk-in guests | "a walk-in is standing at the counter"; "Guests pay by cash, card or UPI" | Guests have **no account**; records store `guest_name` + `guest_phone`; always served by staff | Matches the role model (Guest ≠ login) | Optional guest accounts later |
| A-22 | Notifications | "Someone at the club should hear about it"; "nobody should have to remember" | **In-app notifications only** (polling). No email/SMS/WhatsApp | Out of 12-hour scope | Add fan-out in `NotificationsService` |
| A-23 | Trial session | "might book a trial session on the spot" | Trial = visitor submits a TRIAL enquiry with preferred slot; desk confirms and creates a **free 1-hour TRIAL booking**; no instant self-booking by anonymous visitors | Avoids anonymous bookings/no-shows | Add public self-booking |
| A-24 | Shifts | "Staff work in shifts" | Same-day shifts (no midnight crossing); areas FRONT_DESK/BAR/KITCHEN/SHOP/COURTS; no break/overtime logic | Minimal roster | Extend columns |
| A-25 | Payroll | "employees to pay" | One row per employee per month = fixed `monthly_salary`; no deductions, PF/ESI or bonuses | Brief asks only that payments be tracked | Extend the payroll model (extra columns, multiple rows per month) |
| A-26 | "Share the numbers" | "to share the numbers when needed" | **CSV export** of reports; no scheduled emails or public links | Simplest shareable artefact | PDF / email later |
| A-27 | Scope of the system | one club | Single club, single currency (INR), single timezone (IST), English UI | Brief describes one club | Multi-tenant is a redesign |
| A-28 | Technology | Not specified (team uses PostgreSQL + Supabase + pgAdmin) | React + Vite + TypeScript frontend; Node + Express + TypeScript + `pg` backend; no ORM | Team-familiar, fast to build | ADR-008 |
| A-29 | Authentication provider | Not specified | Own email+password with backend-issued JWT, **not** Supabase Auth | Local and Supabase must behave identically; single role table | ADR-002 |
| A-30 | Priorities | 12 hours, 4 people | Requirements are classified MUST / SHOULD / NICE; **nothing is dropped from the design**, only sequenced | Instruction from the team | [REQUIREMENTS.md](requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md) |
| A-31 | Refunds | "people cancel" | Cancellation inside policy → automatic full refund recorded on the payment; outside → none; owner can refund manually | Money must stay reconcilable | R-FIN-06 |
| A-32 | Self-signup | "Someone signs up at the front desk" + public site with signup | Public signup creates a MEMBER **without a plan** (pays walk-in rates until a plan is bought); desk registration can buy a plan at once | Both entry points required by the team | R-MEM-05 |
| A-33 | Court pricing by time | "Members pay less than walk-ins" | Flat hourly rate per court, no peak/off-peak | Brief has no peak pricing | Add price rules table |
| A-34 | Inventory scope | "always know what is in stock and when something is running low" | One location; stock changes via sales and owner adjustments only; no reorder automation | Matches the brief | — |
| A-35 | Kitchen scope | "the kitchen keeps asking who ordered what" | **Every** bar order (drinks included) appears on the kitchen board; no station routing | Simple and visible | Add `station` to items |
| A-36 | Demo data | — | All seed people/companies/emails are fictional (`@example.com`/`.example`); dev password `Password@123` | Safe to share | — |
| A-37 | Membership payments | "memberships ... to invoice" | A membership can be paid at purchase (payment) or invoiced (`invoice_type = MEMBERSHIP`); the invoice path is for renewals billed in advance | Brief lists memberships among things invoiced | R-FIN-05 |
| A-38 | Reporting windows | "today, this week and this month" | Week = Monday–today; month = 1st–today (IST) | Calendar-based, predictable | `reports.dashboard` |
| A-39 | Data retention | — | Nothing is hard-deleted; no GDPR-style erasure flow in v1 | Financial traceability | Add anonymise job |
| A-40 | Dashboards (UI demo scope) | Business clients are "invoiced"; the brief gives them no dashboard | The hackathon UI shows a business client's **own books** (revenue, expenses, profit/loss), dealers/partners and a product catalogue with CRUD, and a kitchen-manager POS/stock/products area. These run on a client-side demo store; **none of it is in the API contract yet** (it extends A-05) | The presentation brief asked for these views; keeping them in the UI layer avoids changing the frozen contract | If the product ships them, add endpoints/tables through the contract-change procedure in TEAM_GUIDELINES §5 |
